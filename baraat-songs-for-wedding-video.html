Important: Music Licensing & Copyright Guidance for Creators

1. YouTube Content ID: If you upload a wedding video to YouTube containing commercial Bollywood songs, the automated Content ID system will identify the recording. In most cases, the music label allows the video to remain live, but places ads on it and claims the monetization rights.

2. Instagram Reels vs. Long-form Edits: For Instagram and Facebook reels, selecting songs directly from the official Instagram In-App Music Library ensures your audio won't be muted or taken down due to platform licensing agreements.

3. Commercial Filmmaker Deliverables: If you are a professional wedding cinematographer delivering files directly to clients on USB drives or private cloud links, client personal playback is protected. However, if using the film in your public studio portfolio, consider licensed indie tracks or instrumental covers to avoid takedowns. Explore our curated rotations to find songs by baraat phase.

Frequently Asked Questions: Wedding Video Music

Can a couple upload their wedding film to YouTube without getting strikes?

A copyright claim is not a copyright strike. Commercial Bollywood songs usually receive claims, meaning the copyright holder monetizes the views. The video remains viewable for family and friends in over 95% of cases.

What audio quality should be used for video editing?

Always source high-bitrate uncompressed audio (320kbps MP3 or 24-bit WAV) rather than low-quality web rips to ensure the low-end dhol frequencies sound punchy on home theater speakers.
